## About Snowforce Club

### Our Story

SnowForceClub's story begins with five snowboarding best friends, united by their passion for the slopes and a shared dream. They founded SnowForceClub with a simple yet profound mission: to spread their love for snowboarding and skiing to everyone.

Rooted in friendship and a deep connection to snow sports, SnowForceClub was born out of the desire to make snowboarding and skiing accessible and enjoyable for people of all skill levels and backgrounds.

The founders envisioned a community where the joy of hitting the slopes could be shared, where beginners could learn and enthusiasts could thrive. SnowForceClub is more than just a club; it's a movement towards bringing the exhilarating world of snow sports to the masses, one slope at a time.

### Our Team

The SnowForceClub team is comprised of a diverse group of snow sports enthusiasts, each bringing their unique skills, passions, and experiences to the club.

Founders:

- Miracle Shang: With a background in competitive snowboarding, Miracle brings technical expertise and a vision for snowboarding education to the club.
- Charles Xue: Charles, an experienced snowboarder, focuses on creating inclusive programs that welcome members from all backgrounds and skill levels.
- Nels Cai: Nels, a savvy business strategist, handles partnerships and sponsorships, ensuring the club has the resources to thrive.
- Will Su: Will's passion for community building drives the club's social events and member engagement efforts.
- Ping Wang: An avid adventurer, Leo oversees trip planning and logistics, making sure every club outing is memorable and fun.
- Sunny Tang: Handling member relations, Sunny is the go-to person for all member inquiries, making sure everyone feels at home in the club.

## Events

SnowForceClub hosts a range of events to cater to every snowboarder and skier:

- Weekly Group Trips: Trips to various snow resorts, suitable for all skill levels.
- Monthly Training Sessions: Free sessions for skills improvement, open to all levels.
- Competitions: Monthly snowboarding and skiing competitions to showcase your talents.
- Workshops: Covering topics like equipment maintenance and avalanche safety.
- Seasonal Parties: Celebrate the start and end of each season with the SnowForceClub community.
- Collaborative Events: Unique events in partnership with local businesses and organizations.
- Social Gatherings: Regular meet-ups to strengthen our community bonds.

Join our events for fun, learning, and community.

## Instructors

### Miracle Shang

Miracle Shang has achieved CASI (Canadian Association of Snowboard Instructors) Level 2 certification for snowboarding. This qualification signifies an advanced level of snowboarding skill and knowledge, enabling him to teach intermediate techniques and guide students in improving their snowboarding abilities. It reflects his commitment to excellence and passion for the sport.

### Charles Xue

Charles Xue is a distinguished instructor with extensive expertise in snowboarding and skiing. Charles brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to his teaching. His approach emphasizes safety, technique, and fun, catering to a wide range of abilities from beginners to advanced enthusiasts.

Charles is known for his patient and encouraging teaching style, making him highly effective at boosting his students' confidence on the slopes. He tailors his lessons to meet individual needs, ensuring that each student achieves their personal goals. Whether you're looking to master your first turns or refine advanced techniques, Charles is dedicated to helping you progress and find joy in snow sports.

### Paul He

Paul He is a renowned snowboarding instructor, particularly celebrated for his expertise in carving. With a deep understanding of snowboarding dynamics and an exceptional ability to convey complex techniques in an accessible manner, Paul excels in helping students master the art of carving turns on the snow.

Passionate about snowboarding and dedicated to teaching, Paul creates a learning environment that is not only informative but also incredibly motivating and fun. His lessons are designed to challenge students while fostering an atmosphere of encouragement and support.

### Monica Lee

Monica Lee stands out as an exceptional snowboarding instructor, known for her comprehensive teaching style and infectious enthusiasm for the sport. With a strong background in snowboarding techniques and safety, Monica excels in guiding students of all levels to enhance their abilities on the snow.

Whether you're stepping onto a snowboard for the first time or aiming to master new tricks and techniques, Monica Lee is the instructor who can inspire you to achieve your snowboarding aspirations while having a great time on the mountains.
