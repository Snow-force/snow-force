# SnowForce 商城配送与退换货政策草稿

2026-10-01。地址、配送范围和运费已由经营者确认并写入 Medusa；以下时限与退换货条件为本次拟定的经营政策，尚未对外发布。发布前补充正式销售主体、客服联系方式与生效日期，并核对实际处理能力。本政策仅适用于装备商城；活动报名退款沿用原政策。

## 中文顾客文案

### 配送范围与运费

我们从美国加州 Irvine 发货，配送覆盖美国本土 48 州及华盛顿特区，暂不配送 Alaska、Hawaii、美国属地、海外地址或 APO/FPO/DPO 军邮地址。

订单商品优惠后金额满 US$100，享标准配送免运费；未满 US$100，标准运费为 US$9.95。税费与运费不计入包邮门槛。适用的销售税在确认收货地址后于结账页单独显示。

现货订单预计在付款确认后的 1–2 个工作日处理，标准配送预计在交给承运商后 3–7 个工作日送达。工作日不含周末和节假日；以上为预计时间，天气、节假日和承运商延误可能影响送达。发货后可在会员订单中查看追踪信息。首版不提供到店自提或到付。

### 退货与换货期限

请在商品送达后 30 天内，通过会员中心的订单详情提交退货或换货申请。申请需注明订单、商品、数量和原因；涉及破损、质量问题或发错商品时，请保留商品及包装并提供照片，便于核实。

我们预计在 2 个工作日内审核并回复。请等候批准和退回说明，再寄回商品；批准后请在 14 天内交给承运商，并在订单中填写退回追踪号。无需在批准期限内完成送达，但应按时交运。特殊情况可通过该申请联系售后协调。

### 商品条件

因尺码、颜色或个人偏好申请退换货，商品需保持未穿着、未使用、未清洗、未改装的状态，吊牌、附件和原包装完整。室内试穿以确认尺码可以接受；不得用于滑雪或其他户外活动。请使用外层包装保护商品，不要直接在商品原包装上粘贴物流标签。

已使用、清洗、改装或因不当使用损坏的商品，不适用个人偏好退换货。质量缺陷、错发或运输损坏由售后单独核实，不受“必须完好无损”这一要求限制。超过 30 天的质量问题仍可联系售后，根据适用的产品保修及消费者权利处理。

首版普通商品均按本政策销售。只有在商品页和购买前明确标注 Final Sale 的商品，才不接受个人偏好退换货；该标注不排除质量问题、错发或法律规定的消费者权利。不从图册或商品类别自动认定任何商品为 Final Sale。

### 谁承担运费

| 原因 | 退回运费 | 换货再次寄出 | 原订单运费 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### 退款与换货

退回商品收妥并检查通过后，预计在 5 个工作日内发起退款，退至原支付方式；银行显示到账的时间可能不同。退款以原订单该商品优惠后的实际支付金额为准，按适用规则退还对应销售税，并按上述规则处理运费。不按商品现价计算退款，不退现金到另一张卡，也不收取未事先披露的费用。

换货需审核时有可用库存。同款尺码或颜色换货原则上按原订单商品价格处理；如涉及不同商品或价差，售后先确认退款或补款金额，经顾客同意后办理。无可换库存时可改为按本政策退货退款。退款和换货不会在提交申请时自动发生。

## English customer copy

### Shipping

We ship from Irvine, California to the contiguous 48 United States and Washington, DC. We do not currently ship to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, international destinations, or APO/FPO/DPO addresses. Local pickup and collect-on-delivery shipping are not available at launch.

Standard shipping is free when the merchandise total after discounts is US$100 or more. Otherwise, standard shipping is US$9.95. Shipping and tax do not count toward the free-shipping threshold. Applicable sales tax is shown separately at checkout after your delivery address is confirmed.

In-stock orders are expected to be processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Standard delivery is estimated at 3–7 business days after carrier handoff. Business days exclude weekends and holidays. Estimates are not guaranteed; weather, holidays and carrier delays may affect delivery. Tracking information is available in your member order details after shipment.

### Requesting a return or exchange

Submit a return or exchange request from your member order details within 30 days of delivery. Include the order, items, quantities and reason. For damage, defects or an incorrect item, retain the merchandise and packaging and provide photographs for review.

We aim to review requests within 2 business days. Wait for approval and return instructions before shipping an item back. After approval, hand the package to the carrier within 14 days and add the return tracking number to your request. The package does not need to arrive within those 14 days, but it must be dispatched on time. Contact us through the request if an exception needs to be arranged.

For fit, color or change-of-mind returns, items must be unworn, unused, unwashed and unaltered, with their tags, accessories and original packaging. Indoor try-on is permitted; use for skiing or other outdoor activities is not. Use an outer shipping package and do not attach shipping labels directly to the original product packaging.

Used, washed, altered or misused items are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. Verified defects, incorrect items and transit damage are reviewed separately and are not excluded by the requirement that ordinary returns be undamaged. Contact us about defects discovered after 30 days; applicable warranty and consumer rights still apply.

Ordinary launch products follow this policy. An item is excluded from change-of-mind returns only when clearly marked Final Sale on its product page before purchase. Final Sale does not exclude verified defects, incorrect items or statutory consumer rights.

### Shipping costs, refunds and exchanges

For fit, color or change-of-mind returns, you pay tracked return shipping and the original outbound shipping charge is generally not refundable. SnowForce covers standard outbound shipping for the first exchange of the same style for another size or color. Any other exchange shipping charge is explained before approval.

For verified defects, incorrect items or transit damage, SnowForce covers return shipping using the approved label or reimbursement of pre-approved actual postage, and covers replacement shipping. Original shipping is refunded when the entire order is returned because of such an issue. For a partial return, any related reasonable shipping reimbursement is specified in the review response.

There is no restocking fee. If an order originally qualified for free shipping, we do not retroactively charge outbound shipping when a partial return brings the remaining merchandise below US$100. Confirm the return postage arrangement before shipping; do not send a package collect-on-delivery.

After returned items arrive and pass inspection, we aim to initiate refunds within 5 business days to the original payment method. Your bank's posting time may differ. Refunds use the original discounted amount paid for each item, include applicable refundable sales tax and follow the shipping rules above. Refunds are not based on current prices and are not sent to a different card.

Exchanges depend on available stock. Same-style size or color exchanges generally retain the original item price. For different products or price differences, we explain the refund or additional payment and obtain your agreement before proceeding. If replacement stock is unavailable, you may choose a return and refund under this policy. Submitting a request does not automatically issue a refund or replacement.

Approved returns are sent to SnowForce — Returns, 1 Park Plaza, #600 Office 44, Irvine, CA 92614, United States. Include your order number and approved request number. This address does not accept walk-in returns without an appointment. Retain dispatch and tracking evidence; carrier loss or damage is handled according to applicable carrier responsibilities and law.

This policy does not limit your statutory consumer rights. Use the after-sales request in your member order details for support; the published policy will also include our customer service email.
