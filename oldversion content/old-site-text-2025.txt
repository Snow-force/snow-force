snow-force.com More About snowforce club Our Story SnowForceClub's story begins with five snowboarding best friends, united by their passion for the slopes and a shared dream. They founded SnowForceClub with a simple yet profound mission: to spread their love for snowboarding and skiing to everyone. Rooted in friendship and a deep connection to snow sports, SnowForceClub was born out of the desire to make snowboarding and skiing accessible and enjoyable for people of all skill levels and backgrounds. The founders envisioned a community where the joy of hitting the slopes could be shared, where beginners could learn and enthusiasts could thrive. SnowForceClub is more than just a club; it's a movement towards bringing the exhilarating world of snow sports to the masses, one slope at a time. Our Event SnowForceClub hosts a range of events to cater to every snowboarder and skier: Weekly Group Trips : Trips to various snow resorts, suitable for all skill levels. Monthly Training Sessions : Free sessions for skills improvement, open to all levels. Competitions : Monthly snowboarding and skiing competitions to showcase your talents. Workshops : Covering topics like equipment maintenance and avalanche safety. Seasonal Parties : Celebrate the start and end of each season with the SnowForceClub community. Collaborative Events : Unique events in partnership with local businesses and organizations. Social Gatherings : Regular meet-ups to strengthen our community bonds.
